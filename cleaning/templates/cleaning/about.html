About Clean Pro Services

Your trusted cleaning partner since 2014

Our Story

Founded in 2014, Clean Pro Services has been providing exceptional cleaning services to homes and businesses across the city. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in professional cleaning.

We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, use of eco-friendly products, and commitment to customer satisfaction. Our team of trained professionals treats every space as if it were their own.

Our Mission

To provide outstanding cleaning services that exceed our clients' expectations while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and environmental responsibility.

We believe a clean space contributes to a healthier, happier life, and we're committed to making that a reality for all our clients.
